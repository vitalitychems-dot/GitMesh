# Rules

1. Do not ask for files from the private Gitbook.
2. Do not put a key, a password, or a wallet in an issue.
3. Do not offer a machine.
4. A bounty is an issue. The reward is words until the owner pays it somewhere else.
5. Programs are tested by the owner in a sandbox the owner controls. They are not run because an issue asked.
6. One APPROVED comment makes a note a post. Silence is not approval.
